Kundli Analysis

Kundli is prepared based on the position of planets at the time of birth. Through Kundli analysis, the effects of planets on an individual’s life are understood. It helps identify problems related to career, marriage, health, finance, and education, along with suitable remedies.

Grah Puja

Grah Puja is performed to reduce the negative effects and enhance the positive influence of planets. When planets are weak or malefic in a Kundli, specific pujas are recommended to balance their impact and bring peace, prosperity, and stability in life.

Brihaspati Puja

Brihaspati Grah Puja is performed to strengthen Jupiter, which represents wisdom, knowledge, marriage, children, and prosperity. Mantra Jap and Havan are performed as per Vedic rituals.

Shani Grah Puja

Shani Puja is done to reduce the adverse effects of Shani Dosha, Sade Sati, and Dhaiya. It helps in removing obstacles, delays, and hardships from life and brings discipline, patience, and long-term success.

Rahu–Ketu Shanti Puja

Rahu and Ketu are shadow planets that can cause sudden problems, confusion, fear, and instability. It will remove doshas related to past karma, Reduce mental stress and fear, Improve career and personal life, Protect from unexpected losses.

Navgraha Puja

Navgraha Puja is a combined worship of all nine planets. It is recommended when multiple planetary issues are present in the Kundli. This puja ensures overall balance, peace, health, and prosperity.
